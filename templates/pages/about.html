About PathGen

Most learning platforms teach everyone the same thing. We start with what you already know.

Why we built this

Career advice for getting your first job is usually one of two things: a generic "here's everything you need to know" list that ignores what you already have, or vague encouragement with no actual plan. Neither tells you what to study tomorrow morning.

PathGen exists to close that gap. You tell us what you know, and we tell you exactly what's left between here and the career you're aiming for — nothing you've already mastered, nothing irrelevant to the role.

How the roadmap actually works

Every career path in PathGen is mapped to a specific set of required skills, each with a priority and difficulty level. When you complete the questionnaire, we compare that list against the skills you've told us you already know, and generate a roadmap containing only the difference — in the order that makes sense to learn them.

As you complete each step, you log the hours it took. That data powers your dashboard: how much time you've put in, which subjects you've spent it on, and how close you are to done.

What "done" looks like

No open-ended checklist. Once every skill in your roadmap is marked complete, your dashboard tells you so — plainly, with the hours and progress to back it up.

Built with

PathGen runs on Django, Django REST Framework, and a small amount of vanilla JavaScript — deliberately simple, so the roadmap logic stays easy to extend with new careers and skills over time.
